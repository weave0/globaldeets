What this does not claim

Mission Control does not rank sites against each other, does not compute a single readiness percentage, and does not resolve a gap automatically because a probe recovered. A curated gap stays open until a human marks it resolved. Live-probe gaps are generated fresh on every load from the current reachability check only.

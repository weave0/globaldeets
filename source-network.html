Follow the evidence, not an impression

Four relationships that are not interchangeable

01

Publisher & operator

The entity identified in our reviewed publisher records. This may not reveal the ultimate parent, voting control, or beneficial owners.

02

Reporting & syndication

A shared wire story or press release is not independent confirmation. The current network does not automatically trace article-level syndication.

03

Ownership & influence

Common ownership can be relevant context, but it is not proof of identical editorial decisions or coordinated narratives.

04

Origin & geography

A publisher's home country and a feed's routing region do not establish the location of an event or the origin of an individual report.

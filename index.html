How were these publishers selected?

Our World Desk rotates among available publishers and feed-routing regions, within a publication-time window relative to the newest item in the feed snapshot. It is not an importance, accuracy, political-bias, or event-location score. Reports outside that window remain available in the chronological News feed.

How this desk works

  • The publisher comes first. Every story links to the original article. We show headlines and, where the publisher permits, a short excerpt.
  • No synthetic spin. We organize, label, and route reporting. We do not rewrite events or score sources for truth or bias.
  • Limits are visible. Machine translation is labeled, and our known coverage gaps are published.
